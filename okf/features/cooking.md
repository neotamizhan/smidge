# Cooking

Density-aware conversions from US cups to weight or volume via `cookingConvert`.
Each ingredient carries a grams-per-US-cup density.

- Inputs: ingredient + US cups.
- Targets: g, oz, lb, kg, mL.

This is one of the specialist calculators that sits outside the general
[category system](/features/general-converter.md). Output is rendered by
[fmt](/components/format.md).
